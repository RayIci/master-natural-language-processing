\section{What Is Natural Language Processing?}
\label{sec:introduction-to-nlp}
\slidesrc{Intro: 1--3; Terminology introduction: 1--22}

This section introduces the field: what the course covers, what NLP is, how it evolved
from hand-written rules to large language models, why the ``old'' methods are still
needed, and a real research example showing how hard the problems are even today.

This section covers:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
  \item how the course is organised (Section~\ref{sec:about-the-course});
  \item the definition of NLP and its two directions, understanding and generation
        (Section~\ref{sec:nlp-definition});
  \item the five eras of NLP (Section~\ref{sec:nlp-history});
  \item when traditional NLP is still required (Section~\ref{sec:why-traditional-nlp});
  \item a case study: ChatBDI, letting software agents talk in natural language
        (Section~\ref{sec:chatbdi}).
\end{enumerate}
